## What it is

My first game using VR templates, and a humbling one. It was a solo assignment where we had free rein over what kind of VR game to make, so I made one where you're an "intern" to the town's local blacksmith. That felt fitting, since I'd just finished my own internship.

## How I built it

In Unity, starting from the base VR templates, with the game's own mechanics coded by me.

## The hard part

I get motion sickness easily, so testing my own mechanics in the headset was tough. Near the end it got grueling enough that I asked a friend to test it for me.

## What I took away

I had a great time turning my ideas for the game into something real, and it was awesome to see everything come together.
