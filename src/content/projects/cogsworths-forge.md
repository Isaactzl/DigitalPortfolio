## What it is

A school project that dropped me into a steampunk world, and my first time building for Meta Quest. It's a workshop you can step inside.

## What I learned

Building in Unity 6 for VR meant configuring Unity's XR tools and input myself, on a headset instead of a monitor. It was a fun first look at making games you stand inside rather than watch.
