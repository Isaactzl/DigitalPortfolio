## What it is

A 3D tower defence game set in a Singapore HDB estate, where you defend the neighbourhood with cat units. I've always loved tower defence, and games like Bloons TD5 were a big inspiration.

## My part

This was a group project, and I was in charge of the core game mechanics:

- how the cats interact with enemies,
- the health and damage systems,
- special effects like freeze.

## The hard part

Balancing the units and enemies was the toughest part, and also the most fun, because it meant actually playing the game over and over. Fine-tuning took time, and time was something we didn't have much of on this project.

## How it turned out

I'm lucky to have been in this group, and I love how it turned out.
