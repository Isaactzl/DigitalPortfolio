## Why I built it

Honestly, I wanted to ship something people would pay for. Auction-style drafts were everywhere when I started, and I wanted in. Then life got busy and it stalled, but I still pull it out with friends, and this season we used it to run our actual fantasy basketball league draft.

## How it plays

A local, mobile-first game for 2–8 GMs sharing one couch. Each GM gets a budget, players come up for timed auctions, and you pick from modern or all-time player pools.

There are two ways to win:

- **Frankenstein Mode**: build one super-player from eight attributes.
- **The Franchise**: draft a five-player roster, where position fit and synergy count towards the score.

## The hard part

The flow of the game was clear in my head early on. Making it something people actually wanted to look at was harder. I went through a lot of sites to see what grabbed attention and landed on a glowing, eye-catching look, then added sound effects and a decision wheel so the big moments have some suspense.
